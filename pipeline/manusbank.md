051|C|Ask me 3 questions before you answer.|The AI stops guessing. It asks about your situation first, so the answer fits you instead of everyone.
052|C|Give me 3 options, ranked.|You get choices instead of one average answer, plus the AI's reasoning for which one wins.
053|C|Explain it like I'm 12.|Kills jargon instantly. Perfect for contracts, medical letters, tech stuff and anything that makes you feel slow.
054|C|Be brutally honest. What's weak here?|AI is trained to be nice. This gives it permission to find the real problems before someone else does.
055|C|Use a table.|Comparisons, plans and budgets become scannable in two seconds instead of buried in paragraphs.
056|C|Max 100 words.|Forces the AI to cut the filler. Short answers are almost always better answers.
057|C|Write it in my voice. Here's an example:|Paste something you wrote. The output stops sounding like a robot and starts sounding like you.
058|C|What am I not asking that I should?|Finds the blind spot. Often the most useful part of the whole conversation.
059|C|Act as [expert]. I'm a beginner.|Sets the knowledge level on both sides. Expert answers, explained so you actually understand them.
060|C|Check your answer for mistakes before you reply.|Makes the AI review its own work. Fewer wrong numbers, fewer made-up facts.
061|C|Give me the next 3 steps, nothing else.|For when you're overwhelmed. Turns a big messy problem into something you can start today.
062|C|Show me an example first.|Seeing one good example teaches you more than a page of instructions.
